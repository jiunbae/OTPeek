Frequently asked questions

I forgot my master password. Can you reset it?

We cannot recover your master password. If you can still open the vault on another device, export an encrypted backup there and choose a backup password. Use that password to open the backup on your new device.

How do I move my codes to a new phone?

Enable iCloud Sync on your existing device, then choose Restore from iCloud on your new device and enter the vault master password. You can also export an encrypted backup (Settings → Export Encrypted Backup), AirDrop or copy the .otpvault file to the new device, and open it with OTPeek.

Can I import from Google Authenticator or other apps?

Yes — scan the QR codes from Google Authenticator's "Transfer accounts" export (otpauth-migration links are supported), scan any standard otpauth:// QR, or add secrets manually.

Codes don't match / are rejected by the website

TOTP codes depend on the clock. Make sure your device time is set automatically — a drift of more than about 30 seconds produces wrong codes.

The widget shows "No accounts"

Open the app once after installing so the widget can read the shared vault, and check that the account you want is favorited (widgets show favorites first).

Where can I read the source code?

The source code is on github.com/jiunbae/OTPeek.
